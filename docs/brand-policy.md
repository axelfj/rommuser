# ROMMUSER — editorial policy

Approved by Axel on 24 October 2026. This decision supersedes conflicting older language, era-expiry and volume-growth instructions.

- English is the default public language. Spanish is an explicit secondary translation or local communication. Browser location does not determine the artist's identity.
- Electronic dance music is the enduring vehicle: house, techno and melodic expression. Melodic techno, techno, groovy peak-time music, tech house and bass house are compatible creative directions.
- Bios are stable by era. An era changes with meaningful human or artistic evolution, not a release or a fixed 2030 deadline. Dates and release announcements belong in campaign modules.
- rommuser.com is the primary public destination. Linktree is an optional secondary directory.
- Studio works with selected aligned artists and professionals who understand mixdown/mastering preparation. Confirm fit, prepared files, scope and availability before booking. Old volume quotas do not override this policy.
- Keep the existing Noche/Hueso/Rojo palette, League Spartan/Poppins, monochrome logo and original imagery. Official label artwork and platform typography are documented exceptions.

## Stable copy

Costa Rican DJ & producer. House and techno, with a melodic soul.

ROMMUSER is a DJ and producer from Costa Rica. His electronic music moves between hypnotic grooves, powerful bass and melodic emotion, across house and techno. His tracks and sets invite people to close their eyes, move and step away from everyday life for a while.
