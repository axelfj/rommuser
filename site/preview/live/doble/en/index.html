THE ARTIST

Between the body
and what you feel.

ROMMUSER is a house and techno producer and DJ from Costa Rica. His music moves between groove, tension and melody. On the dance floor, they come together in moments that move your body and others that invite you to close your eyes.

The music already exists. ROMMUSER tunes into it and brings it down to earth.
