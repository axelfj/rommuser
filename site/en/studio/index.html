04 / QUESTIONSBEFORE BOOKING

WHAT EVERYONE
ASKS.

How much does it cost?

Mixing is USD 250, with up to 20 grouped stems and 2 revisions. With mastering included it is USD 300. Mastering alone is USD 70 per track. For 21 to 40 stems, or 3 revisions, mixing is USD 350. Ghost production is quoted separately.

What do I send you?

Stems in WAV or AIFF, all at the same sample rate and length, with no clipping and some headroom on the peaks. Add one or two reference tracks you like.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds, for both mixing and mastering. Extra revisions are quoted separately.

How do I pay?

50% to book and 50% on delivery, by international bank transfer (SWIFT) or PayPal.

Which genres do you mix?

House and techno: tech house, melodic techno, bass house and related styles.

Do you take any project?

I work with a selected group of artists. Before booking we review fit, references and file preparation together.

Is ghost production confidential?

Yes, from start to finish. The track comes out under your name.
